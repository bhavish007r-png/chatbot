FAQ

What information does the chatbot need?

Stream (Science/Commerce/Arts), board and percentage, subjects (e.g., Maths/Biology), budget, preferred states/cities, and interests.

Does it cover state exams and private colleges?

Yes, it covers national exams like JEE/NEET/CUET as well as state CETs and reputed private universities.

Is guidance available in Hindi?

Yes, ask questions in English or Hindi. More languages can be added later.

Is my data private?

We don’t train on your personal details. You can request deletion of past chats from settings (if enabled).
